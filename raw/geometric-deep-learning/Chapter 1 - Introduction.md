## Scope

This chapter is the entry point to the Geometric Deep Learning textbook and introduces the overall framing of the field around the "5 Gs": grids, groups, graphs, geodesics, and gauges.

## Why It Matters

The introduction positions geometric deep learning as a unifying way to think about machine learning on structured and non-Euclidean domains, rather than as a narrow collection of graph methods.
