## Overview

Geometric Deep Learning is presented on this site as a unified view of deep learning over structured domains, centered on the theme "Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges." The site acts as a hub for the proto-book, draft textbook chapters, blog posts, keynote videos, an ML Street Talk episode, and linked lecture material.

## Book Positioning

The book page describes the Geometric Deep Learning textbook as an open draft resource for students and practitioners entering the field, with draft chapters released online ahead of the MIT Press edition. It also notes that the material supports master's-level courses at Oxford and Cambridge, and that associated lecture slides may be shared alongside chapters.

The page says the book does not strongly assume prior specialization, but it rests on foundations in vector calculus, differential geometry, topology, functional analysis, spectral theory, and graph theory.

Recommended preparation:

- [Mathematical Foundations of Geometric Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02723)
