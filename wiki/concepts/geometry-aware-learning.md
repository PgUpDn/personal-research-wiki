> This concept page is synthesized across the current source pages and is maintained by the wiki compiler.

## Definition

[[Geometry-Aware Learning]] appears across `raw/` as methods that explicitly encode mesh structure, shapes, manifolds, or irregular domains so learned solvers generalize beyond regular grids. The strongest source cluster is in materials and chemistry, computational fluid dynamics, electromagnetics and optics.
