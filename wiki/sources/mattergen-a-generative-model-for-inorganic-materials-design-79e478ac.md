## Abstract

** The design of functional materials with desired properties is essential in driving technological advances in areas like energy storage, catalysis, and carbon capture [1–3]. Generative models provide a new paradigm for materials design by directly generating entirely novel materials given desired property constraints. Despite recent progress, current generative models have low success rate in proposing stable crystals, or can only satisfy a very limited set of property constraints [4–13].
