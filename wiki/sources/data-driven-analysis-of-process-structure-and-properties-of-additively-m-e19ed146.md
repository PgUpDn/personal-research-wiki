## Abstract

Metal additive manufacturing (AM) is a technology that can be used to build parts layer-by-layer, allowing the fabrication of parts with more complex geometry and reduced costs compared with traditional manufacturing techniques¹·². Directed energy deposition (DED) is one popular metal additive manufacturing process³ in which metal powder is delivered by one or more nozzles⁴. A focused heat source, such as a laser, is used to melt the injected metal material locally.
