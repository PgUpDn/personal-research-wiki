## Abstract

Transformers trained on planetary trajectories exhibit a phase transition: at short context they learn Newtonian force laws; at long context ($L$=100) they default to Keplerian curve-fitting [Liu et al., 2026]. We ask whether Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) can shift this preference back toward Newton. We compare three integration strategies on an identical base model: **cross-attention** (TransNAR-style), where the GNN is an optional side channel; **physics-informed output**, where predictions pass through a learned force–dynamics pathway; and **input enrichment** ("auxiliary line"), where GNN embeddings are added to the Transformer's input tokens.
