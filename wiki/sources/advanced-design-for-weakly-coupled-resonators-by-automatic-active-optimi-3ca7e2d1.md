## Abstract

Advanced design for weakly coupled resonators (WCRs) based on micro disk-shaped structures has been developed using an automatic active optimization (AAO) process with low coupling factors. Previous design methodologies have often used supervised learning (SL) with genetic algorithm for the optimization process but the limited initial design domain in the SL-based predictor often results in local instead of global optimizations. This AAO scheme can generate superior designs in the neighborhood of initial high-density dataset regions and add newly-found designs to the original dataset to shift the data distribution toward a more generalized model to seek global optimal designs.
