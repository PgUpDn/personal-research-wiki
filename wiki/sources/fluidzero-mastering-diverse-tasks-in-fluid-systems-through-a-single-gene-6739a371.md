## Abstract

Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) is one of the important cornerstones of science and engineering, such as spacecraft, submersibles, and biomedicine, which are important to understand and optimize, involving four key tasks throughout history: prediction, parameter identification, design, and control. Although each task has made significant strides individually, current approaches remain fragmented; existing models are limited to their specific domains and lack the capability to generalize across different tasks.
