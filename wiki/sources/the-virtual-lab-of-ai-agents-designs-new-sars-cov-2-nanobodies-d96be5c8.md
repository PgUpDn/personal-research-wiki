## Abstract

9 Science frequently benefits from teams of interdisciplinary researchers1–3, but many 10 scientists do not have easy access to experts from multiple fields4,5. While large language 11 models (LLMs) have shown an impressive ability to aid researchers across diverse 12 domains, their uses have been largely limited to answering specific scientific questions 13 rather than performing open-ended research6–11. Here, we expand the capabilities of LLMs 14 for science by introducing the Virtual Lab, an AI-human research collaboration to perform 15 sophisticated, interdisciplinary science research.
