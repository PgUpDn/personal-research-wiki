## Abstract

Neural operators have recently emerged as a powerful tool for solving partial differential equations (PDEs), including Maxwell's equations in computational electromagnetics. Prior machine learning models often fail to capture both temporal field evolution and generalization to irregular geometries. Here, we introduce a Data-Aware Fourier Neural Operator (DA-FNO) as a surrogate solver.
