## Abstract

Accurately predicting when and how materials fail is critical to the design of safe and reliable structures, mechanical systems, and engineered components that operate under stress. Yet, fracture behavior remains difficult to model across the diversity of materials, geometries, and loading conditions encountered in real-world applications. While machine learning (ML) methods have shown promise, most models are trained on narrow datasets, lack robustness, and struggle to generalize to new regimes.
