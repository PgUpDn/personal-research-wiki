## Abstract

Metasurfaces are planar sub-micron structures that can outperform traditional optical elements and miniaturize optical devices. Optimization-based inverse designs of metasurfaces often get trapped in a local minimum, and the inherent non-uniqueness property of the inverse problem plagues approaches based on conventional neural networks. Here, we use mixture density neural networks to overcome the non-uniqueness issue for the design of metasurfaces.
