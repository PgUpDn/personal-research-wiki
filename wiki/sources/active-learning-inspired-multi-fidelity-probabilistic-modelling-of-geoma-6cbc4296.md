## Abstract

The identification of geomaterial properties is costly but pivotal for engineering design. A wide range of approaches perform well with sufficiently measured data but their performance is problematic for sparse data. To address this issue, this study proposes an active learning based multi-fidelity residual Gaussian process (AL-MR-GP) modelling framework.
