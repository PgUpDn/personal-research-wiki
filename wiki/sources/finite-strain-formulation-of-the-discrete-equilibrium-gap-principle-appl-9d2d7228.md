## Abstract

.** The Equilibrium Gap Method (EGM) is a direct model parameter identification method, i.e., that does not require any resolution of the model. It has been extensively studied in the context of small strains but not thoroughly investigated for large strains. In this article, we propose a novel formulation of the EGM, valid in large strains, and applicable to both boundary and body forces, when full-field measurements are available.
