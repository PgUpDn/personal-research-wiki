## Abstract

** Careful design of semiconductor manufacturing equipment is crucial for ensuring the performance, yield, and reliability of semiconductor devices. Despite this, numerical optimization methods are seldom applied to optimize the design of such equipment due to the difficulty of obtaining accurate simulation models. In this paper, we address a practical and industrially relevant electrostatic chuck (ESC) design optimization problem by proposing a novel multi-fidelity surrogate modeling approach.
