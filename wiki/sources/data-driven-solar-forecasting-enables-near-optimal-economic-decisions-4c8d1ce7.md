## Abstract

Solar energy adoption is critical to achieving net-zero emissions. However, it remains difficult for many industrial and commercial actors to decide on whether they should adopt distributed solar–battery systems, which is largely due to the unavailability of fast, low-cost, and high-resolution irradiance forecasts. Here we present SunCastNet, a lightweight data-driven forecasting system that provides 0.05°, 10-minute resolution predictions of surface solar radiation downwards (SSRD) up to 7 days ahead.
